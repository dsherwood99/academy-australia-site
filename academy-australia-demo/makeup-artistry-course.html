Course overview

This demo product page shows how a beauty education provider can present a polished, high-conversion course page with course outcomes, kit options and StudentPay payment choices visible before checkout.

The course is positioned for students who want to build practical makeup artistry skills for clients, special events, bridal work, editorial looks or as a pathway into the broader beauty industry.

What you could learn

  • Makeup artistry fundamentals, skin types and face shapes
  • Sanitation, hygiene and safe client preparation
  • Foundation, concealer, brows, eyes, contouring and lips
  • Daytime, mature skin, glam, bridal and special occasion looks
  • Specialist services including SFX basics, drag makeup and portfolio preparation

Course modules

  • Introduction to makeup artistry and business setup
  • The fundamentals: history of makeup, face shapes, eye shapes and skin types
  • Sanitising tools between clients
  • Practical demonstrations covering foundation, concealer, blush, brows, eyes, contouring and lips
  • Creating complete looks including daytime, mature skin, dramatic eyes and metallic glam
  • Specialist makeup services including latex looks, cuts, bruises and SFX basics
  • Drag makeup techniques
  • Photos, editing, client records, case studies and final assessment

Professional makeup kit

An optional professional makeup kit can be offered alongside the course, including brushes, palettes, foundation products, primers, liners, sponges, mascara applicators, contour pencils, lip and rouge colour wheels and an actor bag.

Career pathways

Students may use this course as a starting point for freelance makeup artistry, bridal and event makeup, salon support roles, content creation, portfolio building or further study in beauty services.
